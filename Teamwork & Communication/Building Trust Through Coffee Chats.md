# Build Trust Through 1:1s In Your First 90 Days

Whenever you start a new job, people are automatically going to be wary of each other. It's normal; while great impressions were made in the interview process, there's still relationship building left to do. Besides knowing how to do the technicalities of a job, I believe it's really important to start putting in the baby steps to build those relationships and earn that trust. There are two ways I see going about doing that. One is scheduling one-on-ones with your teammates and relevant stakeholders; the other is being reliable.

Don't think of scheduling one-on-ones as trying to become friends immediately or anything like that. It's really just about establishing familiarity and understanding the other person's job better. Also, through speaking with them, you start to get a better picture of how an organization's operations run and what processes have the potential to be improved. Through my experience, doing these early on, it also allows you to understand from the relevant stakeholders you'll be dealing with what exactly it is that they need from you that perhaps was lacking with the predecessor. At the end of the day, even if you don't really have any business to discuss, at least you know you started off your relationship just by being friendly with each other and having that visibility.
