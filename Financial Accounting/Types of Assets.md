## Types of Assets

- Current Assets: An organization's resources that  will be converted into cash or consumed within one year which tend to be cash itself, accounts receivable, physical inventory, and prepaid expenses.

- Fixed Assets: Are long term resources that tend to last more than a year.
  - Land
  - Buildings
  - Equipments
  - Software

- Financial Assets: Liquid items such as stocks, securities, and government/corporat bonds

- Intangible Assets: This type is nonphysical which includes property, patents, brands, and goodwill.
