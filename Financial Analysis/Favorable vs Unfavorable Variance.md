# Favorable vs Unfavorable

A favorable variance is a financial outcome/result on actual revenue or income that exceeded revenue expectations. An expense variable can also be favorable when costs or expenses came in lower than expected.

An unfavorable variance is a financial outcome/results on  actual revenue or income that did not exceed revenue expectations. An expense variable can also be unfavorable when costs or expenses came in higher than expected.
