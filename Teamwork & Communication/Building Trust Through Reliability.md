# Being Reliable Matters More Than Your 1:1s

To me, it doesn't matter how well your 1:1s or coffee chats went; it doesn't matter how charming or funny you are. Not following through on your commitments and missing deadlines, you've officially proven yourself to be unreliable. 

**The relationship (1:1s) sets the foundation. What keeps that foundation of trust together is reliability. By being reliable and having some small wins, it starts to establish your credibility.** 

People become more comfortable depending on you, involving you in things, and giving you greater responsibility.

Small wins are what it sounds like: nothing grandiose, but something that makes things more convenient for the team. 

Small wins can simply be learning-oriented by accelerating your learning of your role and how the organization functions. It could be practical as well; the AP inbox is always overwhelmed with invoices every day. To keep the inbox tidy and organized, create a folder specifically for the invoice emails and create a rule where those emails are automatically filed into the folder.

Small wins tend to have a snowball effect as well; they start to compound. People aren't just hearing that you're capable — they're seeing evidence of it through the small improvements you consistently make.
