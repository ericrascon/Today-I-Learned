# What is an income statement?

Typically, an income statement shows a company's profit and loss over a period of time. Specifically, it shows a company's revenue, expenses, profits, and losses. 

By examining all this you arrive at the net income the company has generated. 
>Net Income = (Revenue + Gains) - (Expenses + Losses)
