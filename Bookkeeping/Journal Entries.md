# Creating Journal Entries   

A journal entry is created for the purpose of capturing specific transactions, which usually includes the Date, the Account numbers, Debit & Credit, along with a brief of description of the transaction. The debit amount included in the transaction must always have an equal amount of credit.

The entry may also contain a unique reference number as well.
