# Types of Liabilites

- Trade payables. Payables that are due to an organizations suppliers.

- Accrued liabilities. These are liabilities that have been accrued, as the company has not paid the expense or received an invoice yet.

- Wages payable. Wages that an employee earned by the end of a reporting period but haven't yet been received/paid the employee, accrued income.

- Taxes payable. This classification can include many types of taxes, including payroll taxes, income taxes, sales taxes, and use taxes.

- Intereste payable. This would be the interest and principal that is due on outstanding loans, invoices or credit cards.

- Deferred revenue. This is advance payments made by customers on goods or services that have not yet been delivered to them. This obligation is converted into revenue once the associate deliveries have been made.

- Long Term Liabilites
  -  Warranty Liability: The estimated cost that a company will have to spend to repair or replace a product during its warranty period.
  -  Contingent Liability: Potential financial obligation that may occur depending on the outcome of future cerain events such as lawsuits or product warranties.
  -  Deferred Credits: Is money a business receives before it actually earns it, meaning on the balance sheet it would be recorded as a liability rather than revenue.
  -  Post Employment Benefits: Benefits that an employee or family member may receive when they retire.
