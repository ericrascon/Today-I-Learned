# What is a Chart of Accounts?

A COA is an an organizations is a listing of all the accounts that are found in the [general ledger](https://github.com/ericrascon/Today-I-Learned/blob/main/Bookkeeping/General%20Ledger.md). Each account will have a reference number attached to it for coding purposes, it's improtant to note that every organization/industry will have it's own version of Chart of Accounts. Not all of them will have exact same coding and in fact some numerical codes might shorter or longer depending on the organizations size. It is also up to the organization to decide which accoung number belongs to what chart of accounts.

A typical rule of thumb is it **highly recommended** to list the account numbers in accordance to industry standards as per the example below.
