One of Excel's more newish features allows users to navigate through with more visibility on the specific cell you want to focus on.

<img width="589" height="205" alt="Focus Cell 3" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ace71d49-a3da-4ef2-a438-87da77bdb8c7" />

You can toggle this on/off by going to View and clicking on "Focus Cell".

<img width="294" height="134" alt="MSArmelle_1-1728332353685" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/072ab0b2-688e-440d-a3d5-989ee5e46d70" />

If you want this feature to only turn on when you use find & replace commands (CTRL F or CTRL H), you can do that by clicking on the arrow to the right of Focus Cell, then select **Show Auto-Highlight**
